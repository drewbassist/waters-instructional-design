CASE STUDY

Moby Digital Archive

Media Ecosystem Architecture

I designed and implemented a digital asset-management ecosystem for Richard Hall (Moby), bringing decades of audio, video, photographs, artwork, documents, and production files into a searchable archive. The system combined Python-based inventory and metadata processing, Airtable, Amazon S3, and a custom GitHub/Vercel interface to support preservation and day-to-day creative production.
