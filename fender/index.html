Designing engaging digital learning experiences for musicians worldwide.

As an instructional design consultant, I collaborated on learning experiences supporting Fender Play, producing educational content that balanced pedagogy, music performance, and digital media production.

The Challenge

Beginner musicians need structured instruction that is approachable, motivating, and easy to follow. The objective was to create consistent learning experiences while maintaining Fender's brand and instructional quality.
