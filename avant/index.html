CASE STUDY

Avant

Global FinTech Training Development

I developed e-learning programs and multimedia instructional resources for Avant’s international training centers in Jamaica and the Philippines. Using Adobe Captivate, Adobe Character Animator, Pro Tools, and Audition, I created training media and supported delivery through Absorb LMS. I also coordinated project workflows using Jira and Confluence to maintain consistency across global rollouts.
