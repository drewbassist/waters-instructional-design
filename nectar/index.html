Nectar / U.S. Department of State

Global Embassy Security Training

I designed and produced multimedia training for personnel deployed to U.S. embassies worldwide, addressing active-shooter emergencies, surveillance awareness, counterterrorism, close-combat readiness, and crisis response. I worked with subject-matter experts and contributors to translate specialized security content into structured instruction, video, and interactive learning assets while coordinating production and deadlines.
