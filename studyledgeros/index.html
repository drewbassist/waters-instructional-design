CASE STUDY

StudyLedgerOS

An Adaptive Learning Operating System

I designed StudyLedgerOS as a unified study platform combining active recall, spaced repetition, interleaving, self-assessment, and progress tracking. Its workflows bring flashcards, practice questions, essays, reviews, and AI-assisted learning into one system. The architecture is intended to support bar preparation and future professional education applications across multiple disciplines.
